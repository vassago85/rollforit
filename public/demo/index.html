How it works

Four steps for the customer, about ten seconds

It sits right where people decide to buy, and it gives them a reason to add that extra item to reach the minimum.

  1. Reach the minimum

    The widget shows how much more they need to spend before they can roll. Gift cards don't count.

  2. Pick a number

    Signed-in customers roll against their store account. Guests are keyed by connection. Everyone picks a number from 1 to 10 — the number is just for fun, and the widget says so.

  3. Roll

    A 3D die tumbles across the card. Older phones and people who prefer less motion get a simple version instead.

  4. Win, or come back

    Winners get a single-use code added to their cart, plus a share button. Losers see what that cart could have won, and a countdown to the next promo day.

Between promo days

A countdown that brings them back

When the promo isn't running, the widget doesn't disappear. It stays on the cart with a live countdown to the next roll day, so every visit during the week is a reminder to come back on Friday.

  • After a roll, win or lose, the countdown to the next promo day shows under the result.
  • Loss teaser: "That cart could have won R…" so they know what they missed.
  • Streak badge on the widget when they roll on consecutive promo days. Local only — no personal data.
  • Add to calendar downloads a reminder for the next opening. No email sign-up.
  • At 08:00 on promo day, the countdown turns into the roll form by itself. No refresh needed.
  • Can be switched off in settings if you'd rather it only appeared on promo days.

Safeguards

Built so it can't cost you more than you've decided

The die is just the show. Every result is decided on our server, never in the customer's browser, and every limit is checked again at the moment of the roll.

Your money is capped

  • Never more than your maximum prize, and never more than what's in the cart.
  • Optional percentage of the basket (example: 50%). Whichever cap is smaller wins — so a R1,000 cart cannot walk away free.
  • Optional daily budget and daily winner limit. When either is reached, rolling closes for the day.
  • A hard stop: if a prize ever came out above either cap, no code is created and it's logged.

Codes can't be abused

  • Single use, and it expires at midnight on the promo day.
  • Can't be combined with other discount codes.
  • Doesn't cover shipping or gift cards. You can exclude whole collections too.
  • The minimum spend is checked again when the code is used at checkout.

Fair and honest

  • Guaranteed odds: rolls are dealt in groups of 10 with exactly one winner per group, so "one in every 10 wins" is literally true. Random (independent chance) is also available.
  • One roll per person per promo day. Guests are keyed by IP; signed-in customers by account. Rolling again just shows the same result.
  • Social proof on the widget once someone has actually won: "R… given away today". Never shows zero winners.
  • The odds and a T&Cs link are always on the widget, including the percentage cap when you set one.

Legal and privacy

  • A terms page is generated on your store from your settings and updated when they change. Each version is kept.
  • Written with the Consumer Protection Act's competition rules in mind. Have your lawyer check it before launch.
  • POPIA: emails are stored scrambled (one-way), IP addresses are deleted after 90 days, and Shopify's customer data requests are handled.

Your side

Everything is managed inside Shopify admin

Roll4It installs as an app on your store. There's nothing extra to log into.

⚙

Settings

Promo days and times, minimum spend, maximum prize, optional % of basket, odds, daily limits, excluded collections, colours, sound and wording. A live example shows which cap bites first.

§

Terms page

Generated and published on your store, and kept up to date when settings change — including the percentage cap. Every roll records which version the customer saw.

✎

Advert copy

Six ready-made versions: short and long social posts, email, WhatsApp, banner and print. Each includes the odds, terms link, and "up to 50% of your cart" when that cap is set.

▤

Reporting

Today's rolls and winners, and every promo day's sales against prizes paid out. Export to a spreadsheet.

◆

Test lab

Roll inside Shopify admin with a fake cart. Force a win or a loss, see the exact payout and which cap bit, and never write a real discount code or burn the daily budget.

✓

Storefront preview

A private 2-hour link still lets you force a win or a loss on the live cart. Test rolls don't count in the reports.

⏻

One switch

Turn it on or off at any time. It can't be switched on until the minimum spend, maximum prize, days and a published terms page are all set.

Before we switch it on

What we need from you, Eddie

Everything else is ready. These are your calls; the right-hand column shows what this page uses.

  1. Promo days and times

    Which day or days, and the hours rolling is open.

    Example: Fri 08:00–17:00
  2. Minimum spend

    What's in the cart before someone can roll (gift cards don't count).

    Example: R1,000
  3. Maximum prize

    The most a single winner can get off their order.

    Example: R1,500
  4. Max % of basket

    Stops a small cart winning 100% off. The smaller of this and the rand cap always wins.

    Example: 50%
  5. Odds

    Guaranteed "one in every N wins", or Random (each roll has a 1 in N chance).

    Example: 1 in 10, guaranteed
  6. Daily budget or winner limit

    Optional. Rolling closes for the day once it's reached.

    Example: R3,000 / day
  7. Excluded collections

    Anything that must never be discounted (prizes then only apply to the rest of the cart).

    Example: none
  8. Promoter details for the terms

    Company name, registration number, address and contact details.

    Needed
  9. Lawyer review

    A quick check of the generated terms page before launch.

    Recommended
